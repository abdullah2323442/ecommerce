## 26 | Four-layer priority | pass
FOR: When several discounts could apply, the system always picks in a fixed order: hand-set price first, then medicine, company, then tier.
DID: Tested live in the browser pane by hand. The price a shop (Karim Pharmacy) pays for Napa 500 mg was read after each step. The owner set a rate for the whole company Square (22%), then a rate for the one medicine (27%), then a hand-set rate for this one shop (18%). Each rate was saved on the owner's screens and the shop's price was read again.
  ok: Before any of them the shop paid 247.50 a box (17.5% off the printed 300)
  ok: Company rate 22%: the shop pays 234.00 (the company rate beats the tier price)
  ok: Medicine rate 27%: the shop pays 219.00 (the rate for one medicine beats the company rate)
  ok: Hand-set rate for this shop, 18%: the shop pays 246.00, even though it is a smaller discount (the shop's own rate beats everything)
  ok: The screens say it in words: "A rate for one medicine beats the company rate. Both beat the tier. A shop with its own rate keeps it." and "Highest priority; overrides tier, company and medicine rates."
  NOTE: Done by hand in the live browser, not by a script. Setting a rate needs the owner's password again, which is a safeguard. The test rates were removed afterwards.

## 32 | Discount order (price → quantity → token → credit) | pass
FOR: Discounts stack in a fixed order: price first, then quantity, then token, then credit.
DID: Tested live in the browser pane by hand, in two real sales. (1) A shopper put 5 boxes of Napa 500 mg in the cart, used their Aqua Token at checkout, and the owner finished the order at the counter. (2) A shop (Karim Pharmacy) returned a box for credit, then ordered ten boxes; the owner finished that order.
  ok: Price, then quantity: Napa is 300 a box, 5 or more are 270 a box, so 5 boxes were 1,350 (checkout showed it before the order was sent)
  ok: Then the token: the finished memo shows Subtotal 1,350, Aqua Token minus 100, Total 1,250. The token is only spent when the order is finished, not at checkout
  ok: Then the credit: on the shop's memo (offer price 247.50 a box, 10 boxes = 2,475) the line Return product minus 255 came off, and the total was 2,220
  ok: A token and an offer price do not go together; checkout says so and the shop's offer order took no token
  ok: Found and fixed: on a finished memo the Total and the Paid/Due line ignored the credit and the token (it said Total 2,475 and Due 255 though 2,220 had been paid). The memo now shows an Aqua Token line and the real total
  NOTE: Done by hand in the live browser. The chain is in this order everywhere: price, quantity, token, then credit. It was shown on two memos rather than one because a shop's order does not take a token (only the public checkout offers it), and a public buyer had no return credit to spend. Set-up by command: Napa and a controlled medicine were restocked, and the returned box was approved on the owner's Returns screen.
