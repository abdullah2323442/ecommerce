## 204 | Then profit at the normal rate too | pass
FOR: The profit is then worked out at the normal rate too.
DID: Tested live in the browser pane by hand. The owner started an offer on Seclo 20 mg (25% off, minimum 5 boxes). At the counter a shop with no special rate (Meherun Pharmacy) asked for only 2 boxes of the offer. The sale was finished and the owner read "Profit today" on the reports page before and after.
  ok: The offer line shows 540.00 a box with "minimum 5 boxes", while the normal price for this shop is 720.00 a box
  ok: Ordering 2 boxes (below the minimum) gives no offer rate: the line is 1,440.00, which is 2 × 720.00, the normal rate
  ok: Profit today went from 0.00 to 504.00, which is 1,440 less the 936 the 2 boxes cost (2 × 468). That is profit at the normal rate; at the offer's 540 it would have been 144
  NOTE: Done by hand in the live browser, not by a script. Test set-up: Seclo had no stock, so 30 boxes were added through the stock service (bought at 35% off, cost 468 a box) so an offer could be made. The earlier offer on Napa could not show this because its price equals the normal rate in this data.
