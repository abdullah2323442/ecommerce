## 21 — Pre-Order

> **[IMPORTANT]** Pre-order is **one single feature** — anyone can turn it on for their own products, with a time set ("You'll get it within 3 days" — 2/3/4/5 days).
>
> Where the order goes depends only on **who turned it on**.

| Who turned it on | Where the order goes |
|---|---|
| **MPO** | To the MPO (for their target products). I can see it as admin, but the order goes to them. |
| **Me** | To me — shown together when I order from the company |
| **Wholesaler** | To them |

### Where and how
- **Medicine** — by company or by product
- **Other sectors** — **by size and color**, on the size that's out of stock
- If a pre-order is cancelled, the buyer is always told
- The seller replies "Order accepted" or "Order declined"

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Pre-orders have limited options.** The product already exists, just sold out — so the choice is **only among the sizes and colors that exist**.
>
> Making it to your own measurements is a **custom order** (Chapter 16) — a different thing.

### Accumulated pre-orders shown together

> **[IMPORTANT]** Paikari and wholesaler pre-orders accumulate with me. When I go to order from a company, **that company's accumulated pre-orders are shown together**.

<!-- -->

> **[EXAMPLE]** I go to order from Beacon:
>
> **My own order** — 20 boxes
>
> Karim Pharmacy — 5 boxes (pre-order)
>
> Rahim Medico — 3 boxes (pre-order)
>
> I can add them and order together, or leave them out.

<!-- -->

> **[IMPORTANT]** **These are not linked to stock levels.** They're only shown at order time so I don't forget.

#### When the shop owner is told
- **Nothing** is said when the order is sent to the company
- **Told after the goods arrive**
- Then I decide — **reserve it in their name**, or put it in general stock

> **[NOTE]** Telling them early would mean breaking my word if goods don't come. Companies don't always supply the full amount.

### Draft memo

> **[IMPORTANT]** The pre-order memo stays as a **draft** — editable.
>
> Later I enter **how many boxes I actually got of each**. Then it becomes the **final memo** and goes to the buyer.
>
> **Why draft:** not every medicine is always supplied. A final memo upfront would be wrong.

On the day goods arrive, when I press "Receive", **the buyer sees it too**. They can watch everything live.

### Advance
- A field for the **advance amount** — percentage or taka
- **Seller's fault** cancellation → full refund
- **Buyer cancels** → by the seller's rules

### Complications and solutions

> **[PROBLEM]** The pre-order goods never came.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** I **cancel** it. The buyer gets a **sorry note** — "Sorry, the product did not arrive". The draft memo turns into the sorry note; nothing hangs.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The time passed.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** It **turns red** and a notification goes out — "3 pre-orders are past their time". The seller can give a new time; the buyer can cancel.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** 10 people wanted it, 6 came.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Whoever ordered first gets it first. The rest are told.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The goods came, but the buyer doesn't want them anymore.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** If the time limit has passed, they have the right to cancel, advance refunded.
