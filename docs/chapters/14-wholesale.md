## 14 — Wholesale and Commission

### Wholesale market

A fully closed, protected section. Only approved wholesale account holders can enter.

#### Category-based access

Each wholesaler sees **only products in their own business category**.

> **[EXAMPLE]**
>
> - A medicine wholesaler sees only medicine
> - A wood wholesaler sees only wood
> - Neither even notices the other's category exists
>
> If someone does both businesses, I give access to both categories.

#### Application and license

> **[IMPORTANT]** Wholesale accounts are given **only after collecting their license and required documents**. The application form must have a **license upload option**.

#### Other

- **MOQ** — minimum order quantity, clear message if less
- **Payment** — COD and advance, **set separately per customer** (whatever way each wholesaler sells)
- **Dashboard** — purchase history, spending trend, **their own grade/level** (shows what kind of seller they are)
- **Pre-order time window** — 2/3/4/5 days, replied with "Accepted" or "Declined"
- **Bulk order upload** (Phase 2)
- **Custom quotation** (Phase 2)

> **[NOTE]** Tier progress is not shown live, **only at month end** — otherwise people would force-buy to become wholesalers (Chapter 07).

### Wholesalers sell too

> **[IMPORTANT]** Wholesalers don't only buy from me — they **can sell themselves** (like the many Facebook pages that sell all kinds of things). This increases the number of products, and I earn commission.

| Section | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| **Public** | Yes |
| **Wholesale** | Yes |
| **Paikari** | **No** — reserved, mine only |

When publishing they choose — public only, wholesale only, or both. They can set **different prices** for each.

### Commission — how it works

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Commission is added on top of the seller's price.**
>
> The seller sets **500 taka**, I set **2%** commission —
>
> the buyer sees **510 taka**.
>
> That 2% **accumulates in my name**.

#### Separate commission per product

> **[IMPORTANT]** Before, there was one commission per profile. **Now I can set a separate commission on each product.**
>
> **5%** on one product, **500 taka** on another — both percentage and fixed amount.

<!-- -->

> **[EXAMPLE]** A product costs 2,000 taka and I set 5%.
>
> When it sells, my side shows — "This product sold, my commission is 100 taka".
>
> It's also automatically added to their profile that they owe me 100 taka.

I can see — **how much each seller owes me, and how many of each product sold**. Full detail.

#### Request to lower commission

> **[IMPORTANT]** Wholesalers can **request a lower commission** — so they can sell the product better.
>
> Where I'm taking 5%, there's a **note field**. They write how much commission they want; I review and decide.

#### My approval before publishing

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Everyone who uses my site — wholesaler or food sector — every product they publish is approved by me with the commission set first.**
>
> No product is published without approval.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Products wait for approval for days.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Show a count of pending products and how long each has waited. Products without photos are flagged separately.

### Monthly commission collection

1. At month end the seller's account receives the statement — **"Sales were X, my commission is Y"**
2. Reply options — **"Paid"** or **"Problem"**
3. **"Paid"** → I verify and approve
4. **"Problem"** → they must write the reason in a note; I review
5. **No reply at all → suspended**

> **[NOTE]** If an order is cancelled, so is the commission. Month-end totals include only **complete sales**.

### Which way the money flows

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Money does not pass through my platform.**
>
> - The wholesaler gives **their own bKash and account details**
> - The buyer **pays them directly**
> - **At month end I only take the commission**
>
> This makes the site feel like their own.

### What the wholesaler decides

| Item | Who decides |
|---|---|
| Product price | Wholesaler |
| **Courier and delivery charge** | **Wholesaler** |
| Packing and delivery | Wholesaler |
| Receiving payment | Wholesaler |
| **Receiving returned goods** | **Wholesaler** |
| Return policy | Wholesaler |
| Commission rate | **Me** |
| Approval to publish | **Me** |

> **[NOTE]** My 2–3 day return rule and 50% cash rule are **only for my own pharmacy products**.

### Delivery and buyer information

**The wholesaler packs and ships.** But the order reaches them **only through my site**.

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Their dashboard is white-label** — arranged so the site feels like their own.

| Information | Do they get it? |
|---|---|
| **Phone number** | Yes — for delivery |
| **Address** | Yes — for delivery |
| **Name** | **No** — hidden |
| Everything else | **No** |

> **[IMPORTANT]** **The most important rule:**
>
> **After the product is sold**, the wholesaler can no longer see even the phone number and address. They are **masked as \*\*\***.
>
> The information is open only during delivery. No chance to dig through old orders and collect numbers.

When they approve an order, the notification goes to that number **from my site** — not from the wholesaler. The relationship with the buyer stays with my brand.

### Preventing customer poaching

1. **Contact details are masked after the sale**
2. **No promotional material in packages** — no cards, leaflets, page addresses, coupons
3. **All communication through the platform**
4. **Penalty steps** — warning → listing removed → suspended → payouts withheld

> **[NOTE]** I'll keep commission low, so the urge to poach is low anyway (business doesn't run on loyalty alone). These are extra safeguards — like Daraz/Amazon.

### Return money

> **[IMPORTANT]** When a buyer returns, the goods go **straight to the seller**, not through me. No extra delivery cost.

| How it was sold | What happens on return |
|---|---|
| **COD** (pay on receipt) | The money went to the seller's hand. They refund it. **No risk for me.** |
| **Online payment** | The money comes to me first. **I hold it until delivery**, then pay the seller. If returned, the refund comes from there. |

If someone doesn't refund, I can **catch it at monthly commission collection**, or suspend.

#### Security deposit (Future)

> **[IMPORTANT]** The deposit option will exist, but **I won't turn it on now** — on a new site, nobody wants to pay money upfront.
>
> I'll turn it on after trust is built. Then people will agree to pay it.

#### Gradual trust

New sellers start with low-value products or COD only. After 10–20 orders without complaints, the limits rise.

### Complaints and support

> **[IMPORTANT]** When a buyer complains, **both of us see it** —
>
> - The complaint goes **to the wholesaler**, who solves it
> - **I also get a copy**
>
> If complaints repeat or aren't solved, I decide.

<!-- -->

> **[DEV NOTE]** Serious complaints have a separate place — how many times against which seller, kept in view.

### Returns go to the seller's profile

> **[IMPORTANT]** When a product is returned, that also goes **into that wholesaler's profile**. Sales go to their account; so do returns.
>
> Their profile shows — how much sold, how much returned, why returned.

### Reports for sellers

When sellers understand their business they do better, and my commission rises. So I show them:

- How many times each product was **viewed but not bought** — they'll see whether price or photos are the issue
- Which earns the most
- Which month sales rose
- **Supplier rank** — who ships fast, who has few returns. Better-ranked products go higher.

### Complications and solutions

> **[PROBLEM]** The seller didn't ship but marked "Shipped".

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** The buyer can report **"Not received"**. Then it isn't a complete sale, and no commission. Repeated → suspended.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The seller buys their own product — to avoid commission or boost ranking.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Blocked. The system checks whether seller and buyer are the same.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The seller is going on holiday; orders keep coming.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** **"Vacation mode"** — new orders off, existing ones continue.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The seller deletes a product that has orders.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** With active orders it can't be deleted, only hidden.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Someone copied a seller's product — same photos, same description.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** A report option; I review and remove.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Notifications are off; an order came and they didn't know.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** An unseen-order count in the app, plus SMS backup.
