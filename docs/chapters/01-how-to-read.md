## 01 — How to Read This Document

### How it is organized

> **[IMPORTANT]** Open any sector and you will find everything about it right there. You will not have to jump between pages.
>
> Each chapter contains: what the thing is, why it is needed, how it works, examples, and **what problems can happen and how to solve them**.

### Reading order

**Read Part 2 (Chapters 03–05) first.** Accounts, pricing and visibility run across the entire platform. Without understanding these, the rest will not make sense.

After that, go to whichever sector chapter you are working on.

### What the labelled boxes mean

> **[IMPORTANT]** — A critical rule. If this is wrong, the purpose of the business is lost.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** — A possible problem. A situation where you could get stuck.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** — The solution, or technical guidance.

<!-- -->

> **[NOTE]** — Something to keep in mind.

<!-- -->

> **[EXAMPLE]** — An example with real numbers.

<!-- -->

> **[DEV NOTE]** — A note written specifically for the developer.

### Phase tags

- **Phase 2** — later
- **Future** — in the future; for now only reserve space for it (database structure, placeholders)
