## 02 — The Whole Business Concept

### What I want to build

A **huge multi-sector market**. One platform, but inside it many separate businesses. Each has different users, different prices, different rules.

My **main business is the pharmacy** — that is the biggest and most complex part. The other sectors are comparatively simple.

### All sectors

| Sector | What happens there |
|---|---|
| **Paikari (Medicine)** | My own pharmacy business — the most important |
| **Counter Sale** | Customers who buy in person at the shop — retail, paikari, wholesale |
| **MPO** | A secret sourcing channel |
| **Public** | Retail sales open to everyone |
| **Wholesale** | Other sellers also sell here; I take a commission |
| **Wood, Clothing, Electronics…** | As many sectors as I want — I can create them myself |
| **Custom Orders** | Made-to-measure products — wood and clothing |
| **Food** | Fast food, including custom burgers/pizzas |
| **Gaming** | Diamond / game top-up only |
| **Help** | Anyone can post what they need |

### The most important principle

> **[IMPORTANT]** The whole platform stands on the answers to two questions:
>
> **1. Who can see what?** (Chapter 05)
>
> **2. Who sees which price?** (Chapter 04)
>
> If these two are not perfect, every other feature is meaningless.

### Current state of my shops

> **[IMPORTANT]** I have **two shops** — a large pharmacy and a small shop.
>
> **Now:** I will put the small shop's goods (worth about 1–1.5 lakh taka) into this system. The large pharmacy not yet — it needs time and money for computers.
>
> **Later:** I will close the small shop and put the large one into the system too. Then the two together become **one single shop**.

### PharmaTrack

My pharmacy's own software. This e-commerce platform and PharmaTrack will work on **the same stock**. E-commerce = online orders. PharmaTrack = customers buying in person at the shop.
