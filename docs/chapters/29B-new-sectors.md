## 29-B — Details for New Sectors

> **[IMPORTANT]** These don't need to be built now. But when I add them with **the sector-builder system** (Chapter 15), the fields they need are written here.

### Electronics
- **Warranty period** — how many months, when it ends
- **Serial number** — unique per unit
- **Specifications** — RAM, storage, battery, screen size
- **Original or copy** — stated clearly
- **Service center** location
- What's in the box — charger, cable, headphones

> **[PROBLEM]** A warranty claim with no proof of purchase.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** The memo carries the serial number and warranty date. Findable by memo number.

### Cosmetics and skin care
- **Expiry date** — mandatory here
- **Skin type** — dry, oily, combination, sensitive
- **Ingredient list** — for people with allergies
- **Authenticity verification code** — to stop fakes
- Quantity — ml or grams

> **[NOTE]** This sector fits well with the pharmacy — the same buyers buy both.

### Baby items
- **Age range** — 0–6 months, 6–12 months, 1–3 years
- **Size by weight** — essential for diapers
- **Expiry** — formula and food
- Safety certificate if any

### Grocery
- **Units** — kg, gram, liter, piece, dozen, hali (four)
- **Fractional quantity** — 2.5 kg rice
- **Fresh or packaged**
- **Expiry** — on packaged goods
- Local or imported

> **[PROBLEM]** Fresh items (vegetables, fish) spoil.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** A "same-day delivery" option, and orders stop after a set time.

### Stationery and books
- Books: **author, publisher, edition**
- **Grouped by class** — school books
- **Set or single** — discount for a full set
- New or used

### Hardware and construction materials
- **Units** — piece, sack, foot, ton, bundle
- **Brand and grade**
- **Separate delivery because heavy** — won't go by courier
- Measurements — inch, millimeter

> **[NOTE]** "Delivery by arrangement" (Chapter 20) is used most here.

### Agriculture
- **Crop type** — rice, wheat, vegetables
- **Season** — when it's needed
- **How much per bigha** — show the calculation
- **Expiry** — seeds and fertilizer
- Government approval or not

### Cars and parts
- **Search by model and year**
- **Which car it fits** — a list
- **New or reconditioned**
- Part number

### Second-hand
- **Condition** — good, fair, usable
- **Years used**
- **Room to bargain** — "Why didn't you buy" works best here
- Original papers or not

### Rental
- **Per day / week / month**
- **Deposit** — how much
- **Return date**
- Late fine

### Mobile recharge and bills
- **Operator** — Grameenphone, Robi, Banglalink, Teletalk
- Instant
- Same structure as the gaming sector

### Services
- **Type of work** — electrician, plumber, mason
- **Price by hour or by job**
- **Area** — where they work
- **When they'll come** — time booking
