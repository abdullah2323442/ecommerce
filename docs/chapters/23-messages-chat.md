## 23 — Messages, Chat and Q&A

### Message system

> **[IMPORTANT]** This message system is **for all customers** — **public customers too**.

**Why needed:** after buying online, the buyer's biggest question is "Where's my order?" Regular messages keep them calm and reduce support load.

#### Automatic status messages

| State | Message |
|---|---|
| Order received | We've received your order |
| Picking | Your products are being picked |
| **Packed** | **Your product has been packed** |
| Sent | Sent for delivery |
| Delivered | Delivered |
| Partial supply | Some products weren't available — detailed list |
| Cancelled | Cancelled — with reason |

#### Where they arrive
- **Platform inbox**, **push notification**, **SMS**, **email**

> **[DEV NOTE]** Which message goes by which channel is **controllable from the admin panel**. SMS costs money.

### Notes and voice notes

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Every customer can add a note or voice note after ordering anything.**

- **Written note** — "Send before 10 am"
- **Voice note**
- **Time** — a time can be given when submitting the order
- **Photo** — if they can't explain what they want, send a photo

### Chat system — complete

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Chat is only in custom orders** (wood and clothing). Not needed in any other sector — Q&A and notes are enough there.
>
> But it is a **per-sector toggle** — if needed later in tailoring or services, it can be turned on.

#### Why custom orders need it
Making wood or clothing requires talking about measurements, design and preferences. A form alone can't explain everything. The craftsman may say "This size won't work", or the buyer may show a photo — "I want it like this".

#### What can be sent

| Type | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Text** | Normal conversation |
| **Photo** | Photo of the space, design samples, work progress |
| **Voice** | What's hard to explain in writing |

#### Special buttons inside the chat

> **[IMPORTANT]** Not an ordinary chat — there are two **action buttons** inside:
>
> **1. "Send measurements"** — opens the measurement form (16.1 and 16.2). No typing, just fill the fields.
>
> **2. "Send offer"** — the craftsman sends price and time; the buyer taps "Accept" and the order is created.

**Why these buttons matter:** they make dealing inside the app easier than dealing outside. The job gets done while talking.

#### Blocking numbers and links

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Phone numbers and links cannot be sent** in chat. If someone types one, the system catches it and won't send it.

<!-- -->

> **[NOTE]** Since craftsmen are my own people, extreme strictness isn't needed. **I can see every chat** — that's enough. They also know I can see.

#### Identity
The buyer chats with the craftsman, but **under my name**. The buyer doesn't know who the real craftsman is, and the craftsman doesn't see the buyer's phone number.

#### Layout
- Like a normal chat app — but **clean and simple**, so both sides can say what's needed
- The order name and status at the top
- **Notification** on a new message
- Old chats searchable

#### Complications and solutions

> **[PROBLEM]** A number written inside a photo — the system can't catch it.

> **[SOLUTION]** I can read the chats, so it gets caught later. Repeated → I take action.

> **[PROBLEM]** The chat gets long; important info is hard to find.

> **[SOLUTION]** Measurements and offers show as **separate cards**, not ordinary messages. A "View measurements" shortcut at the top.

### Q&A

Like Daraz — buyers can ask questions under every product.

1. The buyer asks
2. **The product's owner is notified**
3. They answer
4. Everyone can see the answer

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Public customers don't learn the seller's identity.** By default **my site's name is shown**.
>
> But there's a **toggle** — if I turn it on, their name is shown (Chapter 05).

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Many questions, the seller isn't answering.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** After a set time it **comes to me** — I answer. Repeated → a mark on their profile.
