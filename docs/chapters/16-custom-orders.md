## 16 — Custom Orders

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Pre-order and custom order are two completely different things.** Don't confuse them.

| Item | Pre-order (out of stock) | Custom order |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | A product that already exists, just sold out now | Made completely new |
| **Options** | **Limited** — only the sizes and colors that exist | **All options** — your own measurements |
| **Chat** | No | **Yes** |
| **Who makes it** | Anyone | **My own craftsmen** |
| **Returns** | Yes | **No** |
| **Which sectors** | All | Wood and clothing |

### Who can order

**Everyone** — public, paikari, wholesale.

### Simple for the buyer

> **[IMPORTANT]** The buyer orders, and I **make the memo directly** — wood cost this much, wages this much, total this much.
>
> No complication about estimated prices.

### 16.1 — Custom Wood Orders

#### Measurement form

In the chat, a **"Send measurements"** button. Pressing it opens a form — no typing, just fill in the fields.

- Length, width, height
- Depth or thickness
- Unit — inch / foot / centimeter
- **Wood type** — teak (segun), mahogany, gamari
- Polish or color
- Number of drawers or shelves
- Handles or glass needed or not

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Price changes by wood.** The buyer chooses which wood it's made with; each has its own rate. Choosing changes the price immediately.

#### Also included

- **Photo of the space** — the buyer sends a photo; the craftsman understands. Far fewer mistakes.
- **Chat** — photo, voice and text
- **Voice note** — can be given while ordering too
- **The craftsman sets the time** — "This will take 5 days / 7 days"
- **Minimum advance** — I set a minimum advance on these orders; I won't make something for nothing

### 16.2 — Custom Clothing Orders

Like wood, only the measurements differ.

#### Men's measurements
Chest, waist, length, sleeve, shoulder, neck, trouser length, thigh, hem (mohori)

#### Women's measurements
Bust, waist, hip, length, sleeve, shoulder, neck depth, kameez length, salwar length

#### Also
- Fabric type — cotton, silk, georgette
- Color, design photo
- Collar and sleeve style, buttons, pockets
- **Whether the buyer gives the fabric or the shop does** — choosing changes the price

### 16.3 — Saving Measurement Profiles

> **[IMPORTANT]** The buyer can save **as many profiles as they want**, each with its own name:
>
> - "Mine"
> - "My son's"
> - "Rahim's"
> - or just "Person 1", "Person 2"
>
> Every profile stores all measurements.

Next time they just pick the name and all measurements fill in automatically. No measuring tape again.

**Several profiles** can be chosen in one order — five people's outfits for a wedding at once.

#### Also included
- A **date** with the measurements
- Reminder when old — "These measurements are 8 months old, still correct?"
- Can be **edited**
- Each profile has a **list of previous orders**

### 16.4 — Craftsmen

> **[IMPORTANT]** Craftsmen are **my own people** — not outsiders. They have separate accounts and a separate view.

#### What craftsmen see

| See | Don't see |
|---|---|
| Their own orders | The buyer's price |
| Measurements and job description | My profit |
| Photos and chat | The buyer's phone number |
| Deadline | Other craftsmen's jobs |
| Their own wages | Stock |

**Why:** they are workers, not sellers. Knowing prices, they could take the work outside.

#### One craftsman per order

> **[IMPORTANT]** One craftsman is mainly responsible for one product.
>
> But if they want, they can —
>
> - **Shift** the job to someone else
> - **Take others on as a share**
>
> How they make it is their business; I only watch.

#### Wages and progress — the same percentage

> **[IMPORTANT]** I set the total wage (or both can: the craftsman proposes, I finalize).
>
> As craftsmen finish work, they **enter percentages**. Wages are divided by those percentages.

<!-- -->

> **[EXAMPLE]** Total wage 1,000 taka.
>
> - Karim did 20% → gets **200 taka**
> - Rahim did 50% → gets **500 taka**
> - Selim did 30% → gets **300 taka**
>
> **The buyer sees the same percentage** — "Your job is 70% done". No separate progress entry needed.

<!-- -->

> **[NOTE]** The buyer **does not see craftsmen's names** — only the percentage. Who did how much is internal.

### 16.5 — How chat is used in custom orders

> **[IMPORTANT]** **The full chat explanation is in Chapter 23.** Here only how it's used for custom orders.

Chat is used for three things in custom orders:

1. **Taking measurements** — the chat's "Send measurements" button opens the form
2. **Agreeing price and time** — the craftsman sends it via "Send offer"; the buyer accepts and the order is created
3. **Showing progress** — sending photos of the work

> **[NOTE]** Chat is off in every sector except wood and clothing. Q&A and notes are enough there.

### 16.6 — Advance and returns

- Custom orders have an **advance percentage** — e.g., 30% or 50%. I can set it per order.
- **The advance goes to the craftsman** — they need money to start (the craftsmen are my trusted people)

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Custom orders have no returns.** Something made to special measurements can't be sold to anyone else.
>
> The memo says — **"Returns do not apply to custom orders. Check the measurements carefully before finalizing."**

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** No returns — but what if the work is bad?

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** A **"Work not done right"** complaint. I review — if it's the craftsman's mistake, fix it or refund; if the buyer gave wrong measurements, it's the buyer's responsibility.

### 16.7 — Complications and solutions

> **[PROBLEM]** Which craftsman gets which order?

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** **I or a sub-admin assign it** — who has less work, who is good at what. Automatic assignment would send it to the wrong person.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** How does a craftsman set wages without seeing the price?

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** They see **the measurements and job description** and set wages from that. A carpenter quotes wages by seeing the job.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Work starts before wages are agreed.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** If wages aren't finalized, the order **doesn't go** to the craftsman at all.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The percentages don't add up to 100.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Below 100% the order is **not marked "Complete"**. I see "70% done, 30% remaining".

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Dispute over the split — who did how much.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** The person **mainly responsible** decides the split. I can see it and fix it if there's trouble.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The craftsman forgets to enter percentages.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Regular reminders. No update for three days → notification to me.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** A percentage is entered but the work isn't done — 80% entered, 30% real.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** **A photo is mandatory** with every percentage update — I can check.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The craftsman misses the deadline.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** After the deadline, notification **to me and the buyer**. I give new time or reassign. The profile counts delays.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The craftsman leaves mid-job.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** The order can go to another craftsman. Progress is recorded; the new one continues from there.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The buyer wants to cancel midway; wood already bought.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Cancel **before work starts** → advance refunded. **After it starts** → no refund — printed on the memo.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The buyer gave wrong measurements and the job is done.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** When finalizing measurements, **both sides confirm**. Then it's the buyer's responsibility. The screen keeps a record — who gave which measurements when.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** In clothing, wrong measurements ruin the fabric — stitching can't be undone.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Option to come to the shop for measuring, or saved measurements. Plus a **trial step** — try it on, then finish.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Work done, the buyer isn't collecting.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Notify when done, remind after set days. A custom item can't be sold to anyone else.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Wood or fabric ran out; work is stuck.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** The craftsman can send **"Materials needed"**. The job shows "Waiting" — the buyer understands the delay isn't theirs.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** A craftsman takes on too many jobs.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Their account shows **the number of active jobs**. I check before assigning. However many orders come, I'll hire more people.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Seasonal rush — a flood of orders before Eid.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Look at active job counts and decide whether to accept new orders. Or pause temporarily.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Urgent order — "I need it tomorrow".

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** An **urgent option** with an extra charge.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Custom items are heavy and don't go by courier.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** **"Delivery by arrangement"** — mandatory for custom orders.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** If wood is in stock, does a custom order deduct it?

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Yes — otherwise accounts won't match. There's also a field for material cost.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Is there commission on custom orders?

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** **No.** It's my own business; all the profit is mine. The developer must not treat it like a wholesaler.

#### Craftsman wage accounting
- Wages can be set per order
- Craftsman profile has **"Owed"** and **"Paid"** separately
- Month end, who is owed how much at a glance
- **My profit** = buyer's price − material cost − wages

#### Shift and share records
When someone shifts or shares a job, my screen shows — "Rahim took it, gave it to Karim". Everything stays in history.
