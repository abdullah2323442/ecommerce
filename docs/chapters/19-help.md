## 19 — Help Sector

A separate sector — like a small group. Anyone can post what they need.

> **[IMPORTANT]** **The real purpose of this sector — somewhat like Fiverr.**
>
> Many people who open accounts on my platform are **unemployed students and young people**. I want them to be able to **earn some money by taking small jobs** here.
>
> And those who need people — like **Facebook page owners** or wholesalers — post their requirements here.

<!-- -->

> **[EXAMPLE]** A page owner needs a **moderator** — to handle comments and reply to messages.
>
> They post here what they need and how much they'll pay.
>
> A student sees it, gets in touch, gets the job. Both sides benefit.

<!-- -->

> **[NOTE]** **But it isn't fully Fiverr.** There's no payment system, escrow or rating. It's only **a place to post and connect** — the deal is between them.

<!-- -->

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Everyone** can post here — public, wholesalers, paikari shop owners.
>
> But posts aren't published directly. **I or a sub-admin approve them first.**

### What to post about

#### 1. Need a worker or help
E.g., a page owner needs a moderator. Those who need work — especially unemployed students — get in touch.

#### 2. Can't find a product
If someone needs a product that isn't on the platform — they can post. Maybe someone knows where to get it, or I can bring it myself.

#### 3. Any business help

### Product links in comments

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Product links can be posted** in comments. Someone is looking for a pen — the pen's link can go in a comment.
>
> But **the link isn't shown as a link — it's shown as a product card**.

#### What the product card shows
- The product **photo**
- **Name**
- **Price** — at the viewer's own price
- **"View" button** — tapping goes straight to the product page

> **[DEV NOTE]** The card **follows the visibility rules** (Chapter 05). If the viewer isn't allowed to see that product, the card doesn't show at all.
>
> If the product is deleted later, the card says "This product is no longer available".

### Not allowed

> **[IMPORTANT]** **No photos or anything else.** Just normal comments or product links.
>
> **This is not a hangout.**

### Approval process
1. The user writes a post — what they need, details, contact
2. The post goes to me or a sub-admin
3. Approved or rejected after review
4. Approved posts are published to everyone

**Why approval:** left open, it fills with spam, fake ads and irrelevant posts.

### What it includes
- Post form — title, description, category, contact
- Categories — Need a worker / Looking for a product / Need help
- Search and filters
- Comments and product links
- Option to close a post once solved

### Complications and solutions

> **[PROBLEM]** An old post remains after the need is met.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** After 30 days, the poster is asked — "Still needed?" No answer → closed automatically.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Someone was cheated — contacted from a post, paid, and the other ran off.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Clearly stated — **transactions are not the platform's responsibility**. A report option is available.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The same post again and again.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** A limit on how many posts one person can make.
