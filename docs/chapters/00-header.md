## Glossary of Local Terms (read once)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Paikari** | Small shops and pharmacies that buy medium quantities from me. In between retail and wholesale. This sector is mine only. |
| **Holseller / Wholesaler** | Approved bulk buyers — and also third-party sellers who list their own products on my platform. |
| **MPO** | Medical Promotion Officer — a pharma company's sales representative. |
| **PharmaTrack** | My existing pharmacy software. It shares stock with this platform. |
| **Khucra** | Retail (walk-in, individual) customer. |
| **MRP** | Maximum Retail Price printed on the medicine box. |
| **Box / Strip (pata) / Piece** | The three medicine quantity units. A box holds strips; a strip holds pieces. |
| **bKash / Nagad / Rocket** | Bangladeshi mobile-money services. |
| **COD** | Cash On Delivery — the buyer pays when the product is in their hand. |
| **Taka (৳)** | Bangladeshi currency. |
