# PART 4 — OTHER SECTORS

---

## 13 — Public Market

The part open to everyone. Anyone can enter. Retail prices, the same for everyone.

> **[IMPORTANT]** A public buyer **never** sees wholesale or paikari prices. **Even the existence** of those sections must not show.

### Homepage and browsing

- Sectors as **big icons** — with so many sectors, buyers shouldn't get lost
- **What's available in their own area first**
- Organized by category, search bar
- Filters — price, category, type, rating
- Sorting — newest, price low/high, popularity
- **Personal recommendations** — what each person likes

### Sector by budget

> **[IMPORTANT]** One pair of trousers costs 200 taka, another 5,000. Buyers' budgets differ.
>
> So I will **split sectors by price amount**. When a buyer enters their budget, **that range's sector comes to the front automatically**.

<!-- -->

> **[EXAMPLE]** The buyer's budget is 200–500 taka. They enter it.
>
> Only products in that range are shown. Like a mobile phone shop — "What's your budget?" and then showing accordingly.

#### Premium sector

> **[IMPORTANT]** I set an **amount** — e.g., 5,000 taka. Every product above it goes **automatically into the Premium sector** ("premium stall").
>
> A separate threshold per category — 5,000 for trousers, 50,000 for phones.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** A product is in Premium; the seller drops the price to 4,000.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** It leaves automatically and moves to the normal sector. Raise the price and it goes back in.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The buyer sets a budget but nothing exists in that range.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** **Only ranges that have products on the site are shown.** Empty ranges never appear, so empty pages never happen.

### One product, many sellers

> **[IMPORTANT]** Ten people sell the same phone. The buyer sees **everyone's price on one page**, compares, and chooses.
>
> This is the real strength of Daraz and Amazon. Competition lowers prices and brings more buyers.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Whose listing goes first?

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Not just the lowest price — **price + delivery charge + rating + how fast they ship**, all combined.
>
> Otherwise someone would lower the price and raise the delivery charge.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** How do we know it's the same product? One writes "Samsung A15", another "স্যামসাং এ১৫" (the same name in Bengali script).

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Like medicine — a **central product catalog**; sellers must pick from it. Adding a new product needs my approval.

### Shops by area

> **[IMPORTANT]** Buyers **see shops in their own area first** — faster delivery, lower cost. Showing shops from all over the country is pointless.

### Each shop's own page

Each seller has a page — all their products, rating, how long they've been here. Buyers can follow them and get notified of new products.

> **[IMPORTANT]** **But only sellers whose name display is on get a page** (Chapter 05). Everyone else's products stay under my name.
>
> Another reason for sellers — to show their name, they must make a deal with me.

### Product page

- Multiple photos, zoom
- Variants — size, color
- **Quantity discount steps**
- **Delivery charge info**
- Reviews and ratings, **with photos**, video
- **Social share button**
- **Q&A** — like Daraz (Chapter 23)
- **Price history** — "This was 450 last month". Builds trust.
- **Frequently bought together** — "People who bought this also took this"

> **[PROBLEM]** People who never bought leave reviews; fake reviews pile up.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** **Only buyers who purchased** can review. A "Verified buyer" badge.

### Why didn't you buy — and price offers

> **[IMPORTANT]** When a buyer looks at a product and leaves — a small question: **"Why didn't you buy?"**

- Price is too high
- Found it cheaper elsewhere
- Not what I wanted
- Delivery charge too high
- Don't need it now

> **[IMPORTANT]** If the reason is **"Price is too high"**, a field opens — they **propose their own price**.
>
> If the seller agrees → order. If not → counter-offer.

**Two benefits:** the seller learns why it isn't selling, and lost sales come back.

> **[PROBLEM]** An offer was sent; the seller never saw it.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** After a set time (e.g., 24 hours) it's cancelled automatically and the buyer is told.

### "I was looking for this" button

> **[IMPORTANT]** When a buyer searches and **finds nothing**, they can tell me with **one click** — **"I was looking for this"** (with the search text attached, and an optional note or photo).
>
> These requests collect in my admin panel, so I can see **what people want that the platform doesn't have** — and stock it or bring in sellers for it.

<!-- -->

> **[DEV NOTE]** Show this button on empty search results. Group identical requests and show counts ("23 people looked for this").

### Cart and checkout

- **Mixed cart** — products from different sectors together
- **Wishlist**
- **Price-drop alert** (Phase 2)
- **Multi-address book**
- **Automatic location** (Chapter 20)
- **Note or voice note** (Chapter 23)

#### Guest checkout

> **[IMPORTANT]** You can buy without opening an account. But **name, phone number and address are mandatory**.
>
> Only the password and account-creation hassle are skipped.

#### Cash on delivery

> **[IMPORTANT]** Whether COD is available is set **per product**.
>
> **Cart rule:** if the cart has **even one** product without COD, COD is **hidden** for the whole order.

<!-- -->

> **[EXAMPLE]** A digital product downloads right after purchase — there is no "pay on delivery". If a cart has an e-book and a medicine, COD doesn't show. Half prepaid and half later is confusing.

### Multiple sellers in one cart

> **[IMPORTANT]** **Just like Daraz:**
>
> - One cart, one checkout, **one payment**
> - But the order is **split by seller**
> - Each seller **packs and ships separately**
> - **Memos are separate too** — each in that seller's name
> - The buyer sees "Package 1 / Package 2", each with its own tracking
> - **A courier is chosen separately for each seller**, with separate charges

### After purchase

- Shipping tracking and updates at every step
- **A nice memo** — public buyers get one too
- Order history, wishlist, profile, message inbox
- **Buy again** — if someone buys the same thing monthly, remind them

### Editing and cancelling orders

- Edit or cancel by themselves for a set time (15–30 minutes)
- Not once processing starts
- **Change address** — until packed

> **[PROBLEM]** Old orders hang; nobody responds.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Cancelled automatically after a set time; the buyer is told.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Someone orders on behalf of another person.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** A **"For whom"** field with the address — helps delivery.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** An order arrives when the shop is closed (holiday).

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** The order is accepted and shows **"Will be processed on the next working day"**. (Night orders on working days are prepared the same night — this message is only for holidays.)

### Digital products

- Download link right after purchase, copy by email
- **Download count and expiry limits** — otherwise one purchase posted on Facebook gets taken free by hundreds
