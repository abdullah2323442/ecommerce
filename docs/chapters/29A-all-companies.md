## 29-A — All Companies Sector

> **[IMPORTANT]** This is **not a separate ordering system** — it is mainly **a browsing convenience**.

### What it contains
A list of every Bangladeshi pharma company — Square, Beacon, Incepta, Renata, all of them.

1. The buyer taps a company name
2. Sees **all of that company's products** (even ones not in my stock)
3. Can order from there

### Why it's needed
Many shop owners order company by company. They think "What do I need from Square today?" Opening a company and seeing everything at once is far faster than searching names one by one.

### How orders from here go

> **[IMPORTANT]** Orders from here go **as normal orders** — into the staff verification flow (Chapter 07).
>
> Only offer products are different — they follow the offer sector's rules.

### Special rule for offer products
- Normally paikari buyers can order by strip, piece or box
- But **offer products must be ordered in boxes only** — mandatory
- Each offer product states **the minimum number of boxes**

### Brand filter
Filtering by brand or company is available across the whole platform — not only in this sector, in search results too.
