## 22 — Memos and Invoices

> **[IMPORTANT]** I want a nice, clean memo **in every sector** — **public customers get one too**.

A memo isn't just paper — it's proof of professionalism. A nice memo increases the buyer's trust.

### What the memo contains
- Business name and logo
- **Memo number** and date
- Buyer's name, phone, address
- Product list — name, quantity, unit price, total
- Discount shown separately
- **Bonus on a separate line**
- **Return credit as a minus**, labelled "Return product"
- Delivery charge
- Grand total
- Payment status and method
- **Return policy**
- **Which staff member made it**

> **[DEV NOTE]** Every step on its own line — total, discount, delivery, return minus, grand total. The customer can check it too.

### Mixed offer + regular memo

> **[IMPORTANT]** When one order has both offer and regular items: **the offer items section comes first**, then **the regular items section**, each with its subtotal, and at the end **both add up into one grand total** shown to the buyer and me.

### How it differs by sector

| Sector | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| **Public** | A normal nice memo, PDF download |
| **Paikari** | Two steps — first the MRP memo, then the final. I can edit prices. |
| **Offer** | No MRP memo — directly at the offer rate |
| **Pre-order** | Draft memo — editable |
| **Custom order** | Material cost + wages separate. **"Returns do not apply"** printed. |
| **Food** | In the restaurant's name, my name small at the bottom |
| **Gaming** | Top-up receipt |

### Thermal receipt printer format

> **[IMPORTANT]** Besides A4/PDF, every counter memo must print on a **small thermal receipt printer** (58 mm and 80 mm) — that's what is used at a shop counter.

- A narrow layout: shop name, memo number, date, items (name, qty, price), discount, total, staff name, short return policy line
- Printer width chosen in settings (58/80 mm)
- One click to print right after finalizing; reprints are marked **"Reprint"**
- Optional cash-drawer open on print

### Also needed
- PDF download, email, **send via WhatsApp**
- Any old memo viewable from the account
- Print-friendly format

> **[NOTE]** Works even without a printer — a PDF is made and can be sent on WhatsApp.
