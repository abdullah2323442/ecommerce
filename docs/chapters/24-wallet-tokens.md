## 24 — Wallet and Tokens

### Wallet

> **[IMPORTANT]** Return money, tokens, referral credit — all **collect in one place**.

But the wallet has **two parts**:

| Part | Where it works |
|---|---|
| **Works everywhere** | Tokens, referral — any sector |
| **This sector only** | Return credit — only in the sector where it was returned |

> **[NOTE]** The buyer sees on screen which works where. Otherwise accounts get mixed up.

### Token system

> **[IMPORTANT]** Instead of coupons or discounts, a **token system**. Named **"Aqua Token"** — not old names like "coupon" or "OTC", so people see it differently.

#### Two levels

| Level | Where it works |
|---|---|
| **Platform token** (only I give it) | Every sector. I decide who gets how much. |
| **Seller token** (each seller for themselves) | **Only on that seller's own products**, not in other sectors. |

> **[IMPORTANT]** A seller token is **the same for all their customers** — not different tokens for different customers.
>
> This avoids multi-sector chaos, because each token stays inside its own boundary.

<!-- -->

> **[EXAMPLE]** I give a 100-taka token to the customers who take daily items from me.
>
> If they use it, they get 100 taka off.

### Complications and solutions

> **[PROBLEM]** A token was used and the order was cancelled.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** The token **comes back** — not wasted.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** One order uses both a token and return credit.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Credit is deducted first, then the token. Two separate lines on the memo.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Offer and token together — both are discounts, causing a loss.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** **They don't combine.** The discount order is in Chapter 04.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** One person makes many accounts to collect tokens.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Phone verification — one account per number.
