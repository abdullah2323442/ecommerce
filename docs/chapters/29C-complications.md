## 29-C — More Complications and Solutions

> **[IMPORTANT]** Beyond the complications written in each chapter, these were also found.

### Apps and devices

> **[PROBLEM]** Without updating the app, new features won't work.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Show an urgent message when not updated. Force-update very old versions.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Logged in on two phones at once.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** No problem for ordinary buyers. **Limits for staff and sellers.**

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Someone wants to use the website without installing the app.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Everything works on the web too; just fewer notifications.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Older people can't use it.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** **Text-size option**, simple language, big buttons.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Slow internet, images won't load.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Low-quality image first, good one after. Essential in Bangladesh.

### Products and lists

> **[PROBLEM]** Many photos of one product — which shows first?

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** The seller picks the **main photo**; the rest follow.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Two names for one thing — some say "tablet", some "bori".

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** **Alternative names** linked in search.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** One company written two ways — "বিকন" (Beacon in Bengali script) and "Beacon".

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** The company list is preset and can't be typed. Only admin adds new companies.

### Stock and store room

> **[PROBLEM]** What happens to the accounts on theft or damage?

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** A **reason field** in stock correction — broken, lost, stolen. Month end shows the money lost.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Two staff entering the same medicine at the same time.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Two separate lots, no problem. But warn if **the same invoice** is being entered twice.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Two online buyers want the last 5 boxes at the same time.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** An order doesn't deduct stock — putting it on a draft does. Both orders are accepted; whichever I pick up first gets it. The second is told "Sorry, we couldn't supply it this time".

### Money and accounts

> **[PROBLEM]** Commission was calculated, then the order was cancelled.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Cancelled → commission removed. Month-end totals use only **complete sales**.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** I want to compare two years.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Comparison with the same month last year.

### Security and legal

> **[PROBLEM]** Forgot password.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Reset with a code sent to the phone.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Someone tries to view another's order by changing the link number.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Orders, memos, chats — every one is checked on the server to confirm it's theirs.

### The future

> **[PROBLEM]** Costs rise as data grows.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Move old images and data to cheaper storage.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** One day someone else will run it.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** All settings documented, so a newcomer can understand.

### As the platform grows

> **[PROBLEM]** So many sectors that buyers get lost.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Sectors as **big icons** on the homepage, and what's in their own area first.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** Every sector has different rules — how will buyers understand?

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** Each sector's rules in settings, and shown **on the product page**.

<!-- -->

> **[PROBLEM]** The developer will feel lost with so many sectors.

<!-- -->

> **[SOLUTION]** A clear order — **foundation → stock → paikari → counter** first. Everything else later (Chapter 29).
